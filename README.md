A little game about a cat who refuses to stop walking, even though the floor is lava.

We made it in a 9-hour game jam (Nov 14, 2026), so it's scrappy, but we're proud of it.

## What's going on

The cat wanders through a room on its own. It walks, sees a ledge, jumps, lands, and does it all over again. It has no fear and no plan. You're the plan.

Furniture keeps falling from the ceiling. Grab it with your mouse (or your finger) and drag it over the lava so the cat has somewhere to land. Couches, bookcases, fridges, whatever drops. Some pieces land on their side, some come in pairs, and the sizes are random, so you'll be making it up as you go.

Get the cat to the food bowl to clear the level. There are 3 levels, and each one is a little longer and a little harder than the last.

If the cat falls in the lava, it gets launched into the sky in a ball of fire. It's fine. Probably. You just try that level again.

## Controls

- **Mouse or touch:** drag the furniture around. That's pretty much the whole game.
- **N:** mute the music.

## Little things to look for

- There are mouse holes, fish bowls and snack bowls on small ledges. Grab them for bonus points. The fish also gives your furniture a short boost before it burns up.
- The cat's skin is random every run, so you might get a different cat each time.
- We hid a couple of secrets in there. If you find a cheat code, nice work.

## Built with

Unity 6 (2D), C#, and a lot of snacks. It plays in your browser through WebGL.

Want to run it yourself? Clone the repo, open it in Unity 6000.3.28f1, open `Assets/Scenes/Game.unity` and hit Play. If the scene ever looks empty, run `CATastrophe > Build Scene` from the menu bar.
